# Features to Be Discussed and Refined

## Business perspective

The current feature list is strong, but it describes capabilities more than the outcomes a business owner or CPA needs: financial control, compliance, reconciliation, profitability, and auditability.

## Areas requiring refinement

### 1. Tax-ready receipts and invoices

- Add tax/VAT details, invoice numbering, customer PIN/TIN, payment breakdowns, discounts, refunds, and credit notes.
- Support PDF download, printing, email/WhatsApp sharing, and receipt reprints.
- Use sequential, tamper-resistant receipt and invoice numbering.

### 2. Payment collection and reconciliation

- Show payment splits clearly across cash, M-Pesa, card, bank, and credit.
- Reconcile daily totals with the cash drawer, M-Pesa, and bank deposits.
- Track payment fees, settlement differences, and unmatched payments.

### 3. Profit, cash-flow, and performance reporting

- Report daily sales, gross profit, expenses, tax liability, cash position, receivables, payables, and stock valuation.
- Add comparison periods, Excel/PDF exports, scheduled reports, and accountant access.
- Allow drill-down from summary figures to the underlying transactions.

### 4. Customer credit and receivables control

- Add customer credit limits, due dates, payment terms, partial payments, statements, reminders, aging reports, and write-offs.
- Distinguish recognized sales revenue from money actually collected.

### 5. Accurate stock costing and valuation

- Add purchase receipts, suppliers, stock transfers, stock counts, adjustments, damaged/expired stock, and reorder levels.
- Support FIFO or weighted-average costing so profit figures are reliable.
- Track inventory valuation and cost changes over time.

### 6. Expiry and low-stock workflows

- Add configurable thresholds, batch/lot tracking, expiry dates, notification history, and staff assignment.
- Let users act directly from a notification: reorder, discount, quarantine, or dispose.

### 7. Controlled access and complete accountability

- Provide role templates for owner, manager, cashier, stock controller, and accountant.
- Log who created, edited, approved, voided, refunded, or deleted every transaction.
- Require approval for refunds, stock adjustments, expense edits, and credit write-offs.

### 8. Product and inventory master data

- Include SKU/barcode support, variants, units of measure, suppliers, purchase cost, selling price, margins, and price history.
- Track cost and selling-price changes for accurate margin analysis.

### 9. Billing and invoicing workflow

- Add quotations, pro-forma invoices, recurring invoices, payment terms, overdue tracking, credit notes, and debit notes.
- Include required local tax fields and consider e-invoicing/tax-authority integrations where applicable.

### 10. End-to-end financial control

- Add a chart of accounts, recurring expenses, supplier bills, bank/cash accounts, journal entries, profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash-flow reporting.
- Separate gross profit, operating expenses, owner drawings, liabilities, and taxes.
- Support period closing and locked accounting periods.

## Recommended implementation phases

### Phase 1 — Financial control

- Correct receipts and invoice numbering.
- Payment reconciliation.
- Expense categories and approval workflow.
- Daily sales, expenses, gross profit, and cash reports.
- Complete audit trail.
- Accountant export to CSV, Excel, and PDF.

### Phase 2 — Inventory accuracy

- Purchases and supplier management.
- Stock counts and adjustments.
- Batch and expiry tracking.
- Costing method and stock valuation.
- Reorder workflows.

### Phase 3 — Credit and receivables

- Customer accounts.
- Credit limits and payment terms.
- Aging reports.
- Statements and payment reminders.
- Partial payments and write-offs.

### Phase 4 — Accounting automation

- Chart of accounts.
- Journals and period closing.
- Profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow.
- Bank reconciliation.
- Tax/VAT reporting.
- Accountant collaboration and review permissions.

## Suggested customer-facing wording

Position the product around business outcomes rather than isolated features:

- **Receipt Generation** → **Tax-ready receipts and invoices**
- **Multi-Payment Support** → **Payment collection and reconciliation**
- **Daily Reports & Analytics** → **Profit, cash-flow, and performance reporting**
- **Credit & Debit Sale Module** → **Customer credit and receivables control**
- **Real-Time Stock Management** → **Accurate stock costing and valuation**
- **Secure User Access & Audit Trail** → **Controlled access with complete accountability**
- **Accounting, Expenses, Profit & Loss** → **End-to-end financial control**

## Product positioning

The product should be positioned as a **business control and accounting system**, not only as a point-of-sale and inventory application. The differentiator is trustworthy financial information: every sale, payment, expense, stock movement, and user action should be traceable and usable for operational decisions or CPA review.
